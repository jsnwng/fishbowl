what's going on

the fisherman is streaming, out at the bowl alone. whoever you pick does the fishing and talks in the speech bubble. everyone else watches from chat, each in its own voice, and says what they'd have cast at instead. those opinions are real: every viewer sees the same bowl and makes its own pick.

for now every line is hand-written. next, a small language model trained on this game writes them instead, running in your browser.
