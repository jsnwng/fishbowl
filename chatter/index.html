what's going on

the fisherman is streaming, out at the bowl alone. whoever you pick does the fishing and talks in the speech bubble. everyone else watches from chat, each in its own voice, and says what they'd have cast at instead. those opinions are real: every viewer sees the same bowl and makes its own pick.

on a desktop, the chat is written by a small language model, SmolLM2-135M, fine-tuned on lines from this game. it runs in your browser: a 118 MB download, then a line in under a second on your gpu. the switch under the chat flips back to the hand-written lines.

it doesn't run on phones. on an iPhone, loading it pushed the page past 2 GB of memory and the browser closed the tab, so phones get the hand-written lines.
