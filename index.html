fishbowl

I'm building a game in parts, to see what a model running in your browser can do in one. Each part answers one question on the way. The last part is the game itself: you against a model.

pt.1 renderers the same bowl drawn by six 2D renderers, pushed until the frame rate breaks. pt.2 players a fishing game played by a heuristic, you, two baselines, and a small model I trained to copy the heuristic. pt.3 live the fisherman streams, and the other players backseat in chat, written by a language model I trained.
eventually
pt.4 game you against a model, in the same bowl.
